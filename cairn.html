macOS

Know what you're changing before you change it.

Cairn is a safety-first storage and cleanup tool for the Mac. It explains what it finds, sorts items by risk, and removes nothing until you confirm.

What it does

A Mac storage tool that explains itself

Cairn scans your Mac, tells you what it found and why, sorts every item by risk, and shows you exactly what will happen before anything is removed. Scanning and analysis are free; cleaning needs Cairn Pro.

💽

Storage analysis

Large and old files, Downloads, installers, disk images, caches, logs and the Trash, with a clear space breakdown.

🛡️

Safe, Review or Protected

Every item is classified by a deterministic safety engine. Protected items never enter automatic cleanup.

↩️

Recovery

Removed items go to the Trash first. Browse them, restore to the original place or another folder, and never overwrite existing files.

🗂️

Duplicates

Finds byte-for-byte duplicates using size, chunked and full content hashing. One copy is always kept.

🖼️

Similar photos

Groups visually similar images from a folder you choose. Cairn does not open or modify your Photos library.

🧹

Apps, privacy & developer caches

Uninstall apps and their leftovers, review browser traces by location and filename, and reclaim Xcode and package-manager caches.

How it works

Scan. Understand. Review. Clean. Verify.

There is no "delete everything that looks like junk" button. Every cleanup follows the same path, and nothing is removed until you confirm it.

  • Items are checked again immediately before removal, and reclassified if the disk has changed
  • Unsafe paths and symbolic links are refused
  • The result is verified afterwards and reported honestly
  • Optional on-device AI only explains findings. It can never authorize a deletion
