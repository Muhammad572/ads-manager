About

A small studio focused on shipping

HasTech is a software development and publishing brand that builds and releases applications, games and entertainment experiences. Our products run on mobile devices, connected televisions and the web.

We keep our products deliberately simple: clear interfaces, dependable performance and a short path to the thing you opened the app to do.

  • Products published for mobile, Roku and Smart TV
  • Privacy-conscious by default — no unnecessary data collection
  • Responsive support, usually within 24–48 hours
